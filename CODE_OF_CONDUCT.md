# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful and harassment-free
environment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience or
career stage.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate respectfully and focus criticism on ideas, evidence and code.
- Welcome questions and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Give appropriate credit for scientific, software and data contributions.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing confidential or identifying information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, intimidation,
deliberate misrepresentation of results, plagiarism and sustained disruption
are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `ahiri.adil@gmail.com`. Reports will be
reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may
remove content, close interactions or restrict participation when behavior
violates this policy.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions and other
public or private spaces used to represent the project.

This code of conduct is informed by the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
